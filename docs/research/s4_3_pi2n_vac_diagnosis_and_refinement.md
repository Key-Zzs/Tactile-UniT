# S4.3-PI2N VAC diagnosis and bounded refinement

PI2N keeps VAC as the research question while treating the fixed-seed PI2M B_HVA result as a strong competing control. It does not assume that VAC must win and does not reinterpret the B2 result as a universal failure of contact representations.

The stage separates three questions: whether the frozen teacher carries task-domain contact information, whether Vision-side versus Contact-side readout of the existing shared space changes policy learning, and whether the authorized auxiliary readout or loss scale is mismatched. The policy budget is one matched no-H control (`B_VA27`), one Vision-side VAC candidate (`B_VAC_V`), and at most one diagnosis-routed refinement. No teacher weights, historical checkpoints, PI2B seed expansion, second task, or real robot are in scope.

The canonical time contract is the actual 50 Hz control timeline: `+27` raw/control rows is 0.54 s. The old BVA artifact remains a separate `+16` row / 0.32 s historical result. Every new target uses the exact 40,065 official rows, episode-local `+27` validity, and the same 2,700 masked tail rows without deleting behavior-cloning samples.

The frozen S4.2 B3 VAC bridge jointly optimized its Vision, Action, and Contact projectors. Its loss includes bidirectional Vision–Contact alignment and the optimizer contains every projector, so Contact influence on the Vision path is supported by training provenance. The clean VA-only and VAC teacher packages nevertheless differ in architecture, source data, and optimization budget. PI2N therefore labels their comparison `PACKAGE_LEVEL_TEACHER_COMPARISON_ONLY`; a policy difference cannot be attributed solely to adding Contact alignment.

The tracked preregistration is split across `configs/simulation/s4_3_pi2n_dag.json`, `s4_3_pi2n_diagnostics.json`, `s4_3_pi2n_candidate_protocol.json`, and `s4_3_pi2n_evaluation_protocol.json`. Numerical diagnostics and policy results belong in ignored, hash-bound artifacts. Planned work is not reported as completed evidence.

The bounded N1-R audit completed all 90 development-exposed rollouts for B1, B_HVA, and B2. All nine raw runs passed their structural runtime gates and used the same ten ordered reset identities and fixed first policy input. The frozen classifier nevertheless returned `R_C_UNEXPLAINED_MODEL_UNFAIRNESS` because none of the three models reproduced its first float32 action bytes across fresh GPU server processes. The failed audit remains immutable and is not used as performance evidence.

Engineering repair 1 changed only the production numerical runtime environment: deterministic CuBLAS workspace selection, deterministic XLA GPU operations, exclusion of nondeterministic GPU operations, and fixed autotune level zero. It did not change checkpoints, policy PRNG progression, sampling steps, evaluator asynchrony, action postprocessing, or retry semantics. A preregistered zero-rollout fixture then cold-loaded each model three times and produced byte-identical finite `[30,22]` actions within every model. The versioned runtime contract is therefore `R_B_LEGAL_RANDOMNESS_COMPARABLE_AFTER_DETERMINISM_REPAIR`; every DEV/FINAL worker used those environment settings.

The exact evaluation reset identities were materialized without policy inference or performance access. The official DexJoCo generator exactly reproduced the frozen ten-reset seed-8 runtime sequence before accepting 30 unique PI2N_DEV resets from seed blocks 9/10/11 and 200 unique PI2N_FINAL resets from seed blocks 12/13/14/15. The three cohorts are pairwise disjoint. A scan of 33 historical raw rollout artifacts found 1,061 unique exposed reset identities; neither new cohort overlaps that union.

Both authorized seed-42 candidates completed 30,000 optimizer steps and passed cold-load, finite-array, and checkpoint-identity audits. `B_VA27` used the clean VA-only `+27` target without online H; `B_VAC_V` used the VAC teacher's Vision-side `+27` target with the same online inputs as B_HVA/B2. The preregistered diagnostics did not justify an X candidate, so `X=NOT_RUN_NOT_JUSTIFIED`. DEV completed 90/90 canonical outcomes and selected the sole structurally eligible new VAC candidate, `B_VAC_V`, as `VAC_STAR`; anchors were ineligible for selection. This was a structural selection, not a DEV performance claim.

The pre-FINAL audit bound six checkpoints and the 200 frozen reset identities before any FINAL performance was read. FINAL then completed all 24 model-by-seed blocks and all 1,200 canonical outcomes. Each model used the same ordered 200 resets. Successes were B0 41/200 (20.5%), B_VA27 42/200 (21.0%), B1 25/200 (12.5%), B_HVA 80/200 (40.0%), B2 57/200 (28.5%), and VAC_STAR/B_VAC_V 31/200 (15.5%).

The preregistered paired analysis used 100,000 tuple-bootstrap resamples, exact two-sided McNemar tests, and Holm correction across six comparisons. VAC_STAR underperformed B_HVA by 24.5 percentage points (95% paired bootstrap interval [−32.5, −16.5], Holm p=2.18e−7) and B2 by 13.0 points ([−21, −5], Holm p=0.0102). B_HVA exceeded B_VA27 by 19.0 points ([10, 28], Holm p=0.000463). VAC_STAR versus B0, B_VA27 versus B0, and B1 versus B0 remain inconclusive after multiplicity correction. An independent SciPy/NumPy implementation reproduced all six marginal results, paired tables, tests, corrected p-values, and the secondary interaction.

The frozen interpretation is therefore `COMPLETE_VALID / FIXED_SEED_ONLY`: strong-VA advantage over the tested VAC Vision readout is confirmed in this cohort; the bounded VAC refinement hurt relative to B2; and online H has a positive increment given the VA27 target. The policy-domain VAC readout diagnosis and the full-method comparison against B0 remain inconclusive. These results do not establish cross-training-seed stability, cross-task generality, real-robot transfer, or causal isolation of Contact alignment because the clean-VA and VAC teacher packages differ beyond Contact training.
